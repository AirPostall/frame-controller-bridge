Frame Controller Bridge · ESP32-S3

Set up controllers

Use a Bluetooth LE scanner that shows advertised device addresses. Wake one controller at a time to identify the left and right hands. If a hand is missing, disconnect it from the headset and hold its Steam and View/Menu buttons together until it beeps.

Enter each address as six pairs of hex digits separated by colons. This page runs offline and writes both addresses into your downloaded firmware.

Flash your board

  1. Copy frame_bridge-paired.bin from Downloads into the extracted release’s firmware folder.
  2. Open README.md and follow the flashing instructions for your board. For an existing compatible install, write frame_bridge-paired.bin at 0x10000. For a fresh board, include the bootloader, partition table and paired application.

The bundled flash_args lists the stock frame_bridge.bin. For a full flash using this file, change the application filename on its 0x10000 line to frame_bridge-paired.bin in your extracted copy.
