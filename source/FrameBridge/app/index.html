      Trigger activation and release

      Watch the two live bytes while squeezing each trigger. Select the byte that moves, then enter the level that should press the trigger and the level that should release it. For a byte that falls as you squeeze, use a lower activation value than release. Apply the values, then choose Save to board.

      Waiting for controller

      Bit 7 also reports a fixed click. Choose Use travel for L2 or Use travel for R2 to move that output from its bit 7 click to the adjustable travel source.

      Analog trigger sources

      Choose a calibrated touch surface to vary a trigger from 0 to 255 over Bluetooth or wired Pro 3 USB. In Touch, set Release at for zero output and Engage at for full output. A bound digital trigger press always gives full output.
